# Data sources & attribution

The seeded place data in `0008_real_places_osm.sql` is derived from
**OpenStreetMap** and is © **OpenStreetMap contributors**, available under the
**Open Database License (ODbL)** — https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.

- Fetched via the Overpass API (Kyiv area): named `tourism=viewpoint`,
  `amenity=bar`, `amenity=cafe[cuisine=coffee_shop]`, `leisure=park` and
  `tourism=artwork` nodes, transformed into teaser/full place records.
- `0008` loads viewpoints + bars; `0009` adds coffee, parks and street art
  (90 places total across 5 categories).
- Names, coordinates, addresses, opening hours and websites originate from OSM.
- The app displays "Place data © OpenStreetMap contributors" in Profile → Privacy.

To refresh or extend the dataset, re-run the Overpass fetch + transform and
regenerate `0008_real_places_osm.sql`.

## World cities (0031, 0032)

Generated by `backend/tools/world_import.py` from **OpenStreetMap** (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL)
for Lviv, Odesa, Kharkiv, Dnipro, Vinnytsia, Ivano-Frankivsk, Lisbon, Barcelona, Paris, Berlin,
Amsterdam, Prague, Rome, Istanbul, London and Tbilisi (~500 places). Hybrid curation: only
speakeasy/cocktail bars, rooftops, night clubs, viewpoints, notable historic sites (with Wikidata),
murals, gardens and specialty coffee, ranked by website / opening hours / Wikidata and de-duplicated.
Paid places in these cities are sold through a city pass (`cities.pass_product_id`), not per place.
